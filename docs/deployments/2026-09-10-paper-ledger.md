## Practical limits

The old CRUMBS, STONKBROKER and PAPERDEMO positions lack valid historical entry snapshots/quantities and cannot be sold with trustworthy modeled accounting. Newly approved paper positions use the complete durable flow.

This validates accounting and the reference-price paper workflow. It does not validate token identity, executable liquidity, contract sellability, launch latency, actual gas/taxes, on-chain order submission, automatic exit policies, or a live signer. The documented fixed fee/slippage model is not a measured trading-cost forecast.
